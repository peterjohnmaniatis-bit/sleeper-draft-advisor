A quarter of round three goes to quarterbacks (25%) — three of twelve teams spending a premium pick on the position with the third-lowest value over replacement. Hover any grey line to identify it.

The opportunity is not the quarterback points you save. It is that while three teams take a quarterback in round three, elite running backs and receivers fall to whoever does not.

What this cannot tell you

Three limits worth holding onto.

LimitWhat it means
Sample size60 manager-seasons. The findings that show no effect (waivers, efficiency) are the strongest, because they hold across the whole league. The positional numbers are directional.
Luck is partly circularSchedule luck is defined as wins minus expected wins, so of course it tracks wins. Ignore that correlation; the other three are honest.
Reconstructed scoringSleeper retired its public stats endpoint, so player points are rebuilt from weekly league scoring and only count while a player was rostered. Replacement ranks sit inside the rostered pool, but deep ranks read low.
